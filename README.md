# Experimental BeamNG mods

## UniversalWeapons-Tunable
Based on *Universal Weapons* by stefan750, extended with:
- **Tunable Cannon** (fixed and turret variant): explosion radius/force, muzzle velocity, projectile mass, fire rate and recoil are adjustable in the vehicle tuning menu (Weapon -> Tunable Cannon).
- **Hit registration fix** for all weapons: a ray test against other vehicles' nodes applies impulse and explosion at the hit point, independent of physical projectile collision. In BeamMP only locally owned vehicles take hits.
- Vivace presets `Tunable Cannon` and `Tunable Cannon (Turret)`.

Install: download `UniversalWeapons_Tunable.zip` from Releases and put it unextracted into `Documents/BeamNG.drive/mods`. Disable the original Universal Weapons mod.

Status: experimental, untested in-game.
